      The gap

      You’re Already Inside These Systems.

      Already in the system

      A fifteen-year-old with a casual job already has a Tax File Number, a super account accruing contributions, and — depending on hours and pay — a tax return due each year. None of that waits for a “financial literacy” unit to appear on a timetable.

      Examined vs. real

      School teaches what gets examined. Budgeting, tax brackets and the super guarantee don’t appear on a test — so they don’t get taught — but they are still examined by real life, every payslip, every year.

      Exposure, not intelligence

      None of this is hard. A bracket is just a slice. A budget is just a plan. The gap isn’t difficulty — it’s that almost nobody is shown it once, clearly, before they need it.

      It runs either way

      These systems don’t pause while you catch up. Tax is withheld on your next shift whether you’ve read a payslip or not. Super compounds — or doesn’t — from your first contribution, not your first lesson.
