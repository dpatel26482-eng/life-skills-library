The gap

You’re Already Inside These Systems.

Already in the system

A fifteen-year-old with a casual job already has a Tax File Number, a super account accruing contributions, and — depending on hours and pay — a tax return due each year. None of that waits for a “financial literacy” unit to show up on a timetable.

Examined vs. real

School teaches what gets examined. Budgeting, tax brackets and super guarantee don’t appear on a test — so they don’t get taught — but they still get examined by real life, every payslip, every year.

Exposure, not intelligence

None of this is hard. A bracket is just a slice. A budget is just a plan. The gap isn’t that it’s difficult to understand — it’s that almost nobody has been shown it once, clearly, before they needed it.

It runs either way

These systems don’t pause while you catch up. Tax is withheld on your next shift whether you’ve read a payslip or not. Super compounds — or doesn’t — starting from your first contribution, not your first lesson.
